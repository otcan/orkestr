# Public-Safe Console Evidence

The public product proof uses the synthetic workflow `Customer Order Exception
#10452`. All identifiers, times, account context, and system state are
illustrative and contain no customer data.

## Demonstrated chain

1. A configured intake rule receives a synthetic customer request.
2. The workflow identifies the account and checks CRM and ERP records.
3. An inventory and requested-delivery-date conflict creates an exception.
4. The workflow stops at a Customer Operations approval gate.
5. The ERP update and customer reply remain visibly pending until the decision.

The Console proof shows a named queue, statuses, recent activity, connected
system scope, owner, approval requirement, history, and intentionally disabled
demo controls. Its adjacent six-step state summary is the reduced-motion and
non-animated alternative. The diagram is understandable with JavaScript and
animation disabled.

This evidence does not claim that an ERP, CRM, email, or browser connection is
enabled in every installation. The page labels connection availability and
process rules as deployment-specific. No real deployment screenshot or customer
record may replace the illustrative proof without separate privacy, security,
claims, and publication approval.
