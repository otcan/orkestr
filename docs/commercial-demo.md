# Synthetic Console Evidence

The public product proof uses the synthetic workflow `Vendor invoice exception
· WF-1042`. All people, organizations, identifiers, times, amounts, and system
state are illustrative.

## Demonstrated chain

1. A configured intake rule receives a synthetic invoice.
2. The workflow checks a synthetic vendor and purchase-order record.
3. An 8.4% amount mismatch creates an exception.
4. The workflow stops at a Finance Operations approval gate.
5. The final post-or-return step is visibly pending until the decision.

The Console proof shows a named queue, statuses, recent activity, connected
system scope, owner, approval requirement, history, and intentionally disabled
demo controls. Its adjacent four-caption walkthrough is the reduced-motion and
non-animated alternative. The diagram is understandable with JavaScript and
animation disabled.

This evidence does not claim that an ERP or invoice adapter is enabled in every
installation. The page labels connector availability and workflow configuration
as deployment-specific. No real deployment screenshot or customer record may
replace the synthetic proof without separate privacy, security, claims, and
publication approval.
