# Commercial SEO Plan

Organic search should produce relevant Project Discovery conversations, not
undifferentiated traffic.

## Search position

The homepage owns **Custom Business Software & Automation** and the proposition that
Orkestr builds and operates software that does work for businesses. Initial
high-intent themes include:

- business website and e-commerce development
- B2B customer portals and internal applications
- legacy system modernization and replacement
- tender, grant, RFP, and opportunity monitoring
- web data collection and change monitoring
- authorized browser and web workflow automation
- AI workflow and operations automation
- email, document, ERP, CRM, and internal process automation

Use these phrases only where a page answers the intent. Do not create thin
keyword pages or market scraping as the product.

## Language and market architecture

- English remains at the root URL.
- German commercial pages live under `/de` and use DACH buying language.
- Turkish commercial pages live under `/tr` and use native Turkish buying
  language.
- Every translated page has its own canonical URL and reciprocal `en`, `de-DE`,
  `tr-TR`, and `x-default` alternates.
- Language selectors use ordinary crawlable links. Do not redirect by IP or
  browser language.
- Translate the requirement and commercial context, not only navigation labels.

The first localized surface includes the homepage, service overview, five
solution pages, project intake, security, deployment, and Team. English-only
legal, developer, and specialized workflow pages remain available at their
existing canonical URLs until they have a complete localized equivalent.

## Page responsibilities

- `/`: broad proposition, three service groups, examples, four-step delivery,
  one platform proof, and booking conversion
- `/use-cases`: solution overview and internal links
- five solution routes: focused intent, outcomes, delivery, and boundaries
- `/project`: general inquiry conversion
- `/automation` and `/workflow`: specialized workflow-automation intent
- `/security`, `/deployment`, `/developers`: procurement and technical evidence
- `/team`, `/de/team`, `/tr/ekip`: one accountable founder, Oğuzcan Ünver; do
  not imply a larger permanent team

Every indexable page has one H1, unique title and description, canonical URL,
social metadata, factual structured data, and useful internal links. Evidence is
limited to labelled product demonstrations, public code, tests, documentation,
and approved case studies.

Primary measurement is relevant Project Discovery submissions. Supporting
measures are project-call clicks, solution visits, short-form starts, optional
detail expansion, successful submissions, scheduling clicks, workflow-audit conversions, and organic
conversion by landing page. Analytics remain allowlisted and metadata-only.

The XML sitemap contains index-worthy commercial and technical evidence pages,
localized alternates, and meaningful last-modified dates. Conversion-only,
personal beta, support, and legal utility routes remain reachable but are not
listed. The personal beta is explicitly `noindex,follow` and is not part of the
commercial language architecture.

Case studies must be based on approved, truthful deployment evidence. Never
invent customers, team members, credentials, reviews, or performance metrics to
fill an SEO page.
